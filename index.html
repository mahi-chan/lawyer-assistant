What Release 1 is

A chat assistant for lawyers, firms and businesses. It answers questions on Bangladeshi land law and cites the Act and section behind every claim. Each case lives in its own Matter, with its files and its chats.

Did the 2019 sale need a registered deed?
Yes. A sale of immovable property is made by a registered instrument TPA 1882, s. 54, and a deed that transfers land is one of the documents that must be registered Registration Act 1908, s. 17. The deed in this Matter carries a sub-registry endorsement on its first page Sale deed (2019).pdf, p. 1.

  • Registration Act, 1908s. 17

One matter, cited answers

How a matter works

  1. One minute

    First, open a Matter: client, parties, case number, court, key dates.

  2. A few minutes

    Then add the case files. Scans are read with OCR and indexed for this Matter only.

  3. As long as the case runs

    Then ask, in as many chats as the case needs. Every answer cites the Act and section, or the file and page.

  4. One click

    Last, share the Matter with colleagues. The log shows who opened it.

Behind each answer, the assistant searches the law and the Matter's files, writes only from what it found, and checks that every citation came from those results. If the law it holds does not answer the question, it says so and suggests a lawyer.
